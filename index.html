— The Story —

A World of
Demons &
Blades

Tanjiro Kamado's peaceful life is shattered when his family is attacked, leaving his sister Nezuko transformed into a demon. Determined to find a way to restore her humanity, Tanjiro enters the dangerous world of demon hunters — the Demon Slayer Corps.

Character 01

Tanjiro Kamado

Kind-hearted and determined, Tanjiro wields Water Breathing and the rare Hinokami Kagura flame technique.

⛩ Water Breathing · Hinokami Kagura
Character 02

Nezuko Kamado

A demon who retained her humanity, Nezuko possesses the rare Blood Demon Art and fights to protect her brother.

🌸 Blood Demon Art · Exploding Blood

⚡ Thunder Breathing · Thunderclap and Flash

💥 Sound Breathing · Constant Slashing
